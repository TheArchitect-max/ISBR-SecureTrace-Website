Assurance architecture

Each layer has a narrow responsibility. Trust is not inherited automatically, and signed artifacts do not become scientific, legal or regulatory proof simply because they are cryptographically valid.

Validation boundary

Precise claims. Explicit limits.

SecureTrace is a software and evidence-governance platform. Its verification layers can establish integrity, reproducibility and policy compliance of digital records. They do not automatically establish biological efficacy, legal title, payment settlement, patent ownership or regulatory approval.
